Took me almost seven years to finally conjure enough determination to open this blog. I spent years overthinking what was worth writing, worrying if it would be meaningful, or if I even knew anything anyone else would look up to. Eventually, I ran out of reasons not to and got fed up with the constant second-guessing. I'll probably forget I even have this thing in two weeks, so I'm just writing whenever I feel like it.

I'm a malware analyst and reverse engineer with 6+ years of experience. Since no one seems to need a malware person nowadays, I've dedicated my time to hobby projects I've procrastinated on for too long. I do as I like now, so the topics here will hopefully be varied. I used to be scared of becoming a "jack of all trades, master of none"—well, I guess it's a self-fulfilling prophecy at this point.

Feedback is appreciated. Hopefully, the bits and pieces I drop here can actually help someone out in some corner of the world.
